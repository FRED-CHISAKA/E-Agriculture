The climate

Nyanza is the coldest region in Kenya. It experiences an average high temperature of only 24 degrees centigrade. Several months of the year it is warm to hot at temperatures continuously above 25 degrees centigrade, sometimes up to 27 degrees.

Some areas like Ahero are water catchment areas favouring rice plantation.

The Soil

Nyanza Kenya is one of the fertile regions in Kenya. This makes it an Agro-Ecological zone. It contributes a lot in this county's GDP in the agricultural sector.

The map shows distribution of various soil types in Nyanza region of Kenya. With those soil types and nyanza being a moderate rainfall area, Western Kenya has an agricultural fortune.
