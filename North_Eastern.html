north eastern Region Crops

north eastern is highly arid and semi arid region. the vegetation cover is very low and made up of sisal, acacia shrubs and cactus. The people who live in the area are from diverse ethnic groups that coexist and work together in farmer groups. Maize is the main crop grown. other crops that do well are beans, cassava, sorghum, pigeon peas and millet.

There some agricultural companies and factories that aid farmers from the region by buying their products and offering additional services like market.

The climate

North-Eastern Kenya is the hotest region in Kenya. It experiences an average daily high temperature of 35 degrees centigrade. It is yearlong warm or hot.

May is the the sunniest month in the greater North-Eastern Region. In April, the sun shines the shortest but with minimal or no rain.

The Soil

North Eastern Kenya is mostly dry through out the year. Despite the dry conditions, there are places where irrigation is practised and those near water sources. Soils in those regions are able to retain water for plants.

The map shows distribution of various soil types in Kenya, that includes north eastern region. With those soil types and north eastern being a low rainfall area, productivity can still be improved by use of certain fertilisers and irrigation.
