Rift Valley Region Crops

Rift Valley region is one of the region where agriculture is for ts people. The climate and soils favours its many residents, both small scale and large scale farmers to yield as much as they can. The region is a moderate rainfall area giving an upper hand to the farmers to maximize on their agricultural production

There are many agricultural companies and factories that aim the many farmers by buying their products and offering additional services like ready market.

The Soil

Western Kenya is one of the fertile regions in Kenya. This makes it an Agro-Ecological zone. It contributes a lot in this county's GDP in the agricultural sector.

The map shows distribution of various oil types in Western and Nyanza regions of Kenya. With those soil types and Western being a moderate rainfall area, Western Kenya has an agricultural fortune.
